GO FISH RULES
=============

Goal: Collect the most sets of four cards of the same rank (like four Aces, four Kings, etc.)

Setup:
- Each player gets 7 cards from a shuffled deck
- Remaining cards form the "pond" (draw pile)

How to Play:
1. Players take turns asking each other for cards of a specific rank
   Example: "Do you have any Aces?"
   
2. If the other player has cards of that rank:
   - They must give you ALL cards of that rank
   - You get another turn
   
3. If the other player doesn't have any cards of that rank:
   - They say "Go Fish!"
   - You draw a card from the pond
   - Your turn ends
   
4. When you collect four cards of the same rank, you lay them down as a "book"

5. Game ends when either:
   - All books have been collected, OR
   - The pond runs out of cards

Winning:
The player with the most books (sets of four) wins!
